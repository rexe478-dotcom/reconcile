# Design system
Direction: minimal, editorial, trustworthy utility app. No generic AI dashboard look.

Tokens:
- background #FFFFFF; surface #F7F7F5; subtle surface #FAFAF9
- primary text #171717; secondary #737373; muted #A3A3A3
- border #E5E5E5; accent #66764F; accent surface #F0F2EB
- error #B42318; warning #9A6700; success #3F6B45

Use Inter or platform Roboto. Spacing scale 4/8/12/16/20/24/32/40 dp. Phone side padding 20–24 dp, component padding about 16 dp, touch targets at least 48 dp. Use subtle borders and restrained radii. No gradients, neon, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, 3D AI art, emoji icons, or heavy shadows. Design phone-first, then tablet with sensible max-widths.
